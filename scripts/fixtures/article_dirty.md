# The Price of Thirst

Water rights data is fragmented by design — no MLS equivalent exists.

This is an unprecedented shift in how we price a resource. Here's the breakdown:

The CME launched futures on a California water index in March 2026. The index tracks
five basins. Trading volume stayed thin through the first quarter. Prices moved with
snowpack reports rather than with demand.
