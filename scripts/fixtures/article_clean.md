# The Price of Thirst

Disclaimer: the author is not a water lawyer. This is commentary and analysis, not expert
assessment. Model projections are not predictions. Verify against primary sources.

Water rights data is fragmented by design, with no MLS equivalent.

The CME launched futures on a California water index in March 2026. Volume stayed thin.
Prices moved with snowpack reports rather than with demand, which is what you would expect
from an instrument whose underlying asset cannot be delivered across a basin boundary
without a regulator signing off on the transfer first.

Sources: https://www.cftc.gov/PressRoom/PressReleases/8394-26 and
https://waterboards.ca.gov/waterrights/water_issues/programs/ewrims/
